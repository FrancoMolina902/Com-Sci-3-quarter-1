**Scenario**

The PSHS school canteen is small and often gets crowded during lunch break. Students line up to buy food, but the process is slow because:

Some students take too long to decide what to order.
The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
There is no system to track which food items are running out.
Your group’s task is to decompose this problem into smaller, manageable parts that could be solved with computational thinking (CT) Skills.

**Step 1: Identify the Big Problem**

Main Problem: The school canteen’s lunch service is slow and inefficient because ordering, payment, and monitoring of food supplies are done manually,
and the canteen is usually packed and crowded during lunch.

**Step 2: Identify three to four Sub-Problems**
Please list possible sub-problems:

1. Students take too long deciding what to order, so they end up holding up the line

2. The cashier needs to compute each order manually, which can take a while during lunch/break hours

3. The lack of an automated inventory tracker means the stock needs to be manually checked regularly, which can be time-consuming when many students are buying food

4.  It will be hard for students to maneuver since there will be many people wanting to buy from the canteen

**Step 3: Define Computational Thinking Approaches**
For each sub-problem, apply CT skills:

1. Sub-Problem:
   Students take too long deciding what to order, so they end up holding up the line

   CT Skill: Pattern Recognition

   Example Solution: Post a clear menu with food choices and prices so students can decide what they want before reaching the cashier.

2. Sub-Problem:
   The cashier needs to compute each order manually, which can take a while during lunch/break hours

   CT Skill: Automation

   Example Solution: Use a simple system that automatically adds up the order and calculates the change.

3. Sub-Problem:
   The lack of an automated inventory tracker means the stock needs to be manually checked regularly, which can be time-consuming when many students are buying       food

   CT Skill: Decomposition

   Example Solution: Use a system that tracks how much food is left and alerts when an item is running low.

4. Sub-Problem:
   It will be hard for students to maneuver since there will be many people wanting to buy from the canteen

   CT Skill: Algorithmic Thinking

   Example Solution: Set up clear lines and spaces for entering and leaving so students can move around more easily.
   
 **Step 4: Draw a flowchart or write a pseudocode for the identified sub-problem**
